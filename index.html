Forecast data from the NESO Carbon Intensity API (CC BY 4.0). Regional forecasts are half-hourly, Great Britain only, and are revised every 30 minutes, so check again before you press start.

Prices come from Octopus Energy’s public tariff data for your region, including VAT, excluding the standing charge. Agile prices for the next day usually appear in the late afternoon; Tracker is one price per day.

Appliance profiles are typical shapes: heating bursts stay at the start and end of a cycle and the quiet middle stretches with run time. Bar colours compare each half-hour with the rest of your day.
